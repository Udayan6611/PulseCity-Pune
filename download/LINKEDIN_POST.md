**Body:**

Cities are chaos — endless attractions, hidden gems, traffic snarls, sudden weather shifts, and safety concerns you only learn about after the fact.

For the PromptWars Hackathon, I wanted to fix this. So I built **Sheher** (शहर — Hindi/Urdu for "the city") — an AI-powered smart city OS that turns scattered urban data into actionable insights.

🗺️ **Interactive city maps** with safety-aware markers across Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Jaipur & Kolkata
🏛️ **Heritage & culture timeline** — every Mughal, British, and Rajput site with era and significance
🛡️ **Safety tab** — unsafe zones, accident hotspots, and AI-analyzed citizen reports
📊 **Compare tab** — radar + bar charts ranking cities on safety, cleanliness, affordability, AQI, transit & culture
🤖 **AI Insights** — real-time-style weather, traffic, and contextual recommendations generated by Z.ai's GLM model
💬 **Sheher AI assistant** — voice-enabled chat that knows each city's attractions, safer routes, and best visiting times
📝 **Citizen reports** — submit traffic/safety/cleanliness issues with GPS, severity, and (soon) voice notes

**Tech stack:**
- Next.js 16 + TypeScript
- Leaflet + OpenStreetMap
- Prisma + SQLite
- z-ai-web-dev-sdk (AI chat, insights, safer routing, report analysis)
- Recharts + Framer Motion + shadcn/ui

Every AI call happens server-side. The assistant is trained on curated city data — ask it "What's the safest route from Gateway of India to Bandra at 11pm?" and it tells you which lanes to avoid.

**Why "Sheher"?** It's Hindi/Urdu for "the city" — rooted in the place where this app belongs. A name that doesn't pretend to be Silicon Valley. It's from the streets it serves.
